PROJECT SPECIFICATIONS

Tayyab CCTV Camera Portal

ACTIVE PRODUCTION SYSTEM

A bespoke business management web application engineered for CCTV shop operations. It centralizes store operations, product tracking, billing workflows, and financial account ledgers.

01 — Inventory and Product Management

Real-time stock catalog, item tracking, low stock monitoring, and structured product category management for cameras, DVRs, and cabling.

02 — Billing and Invoicing

Point-of-sale customer billing, invoice generation, customized itemized charges, and printable payment receipt records.

03 — Customer Ledgers and Payments

Complete customer account balance sheets, credit logs, installment records, and client payment ledger tracking.

04 — Expenses and Business Income

Dedicated logging of store overheads, operational expenses, shop revenues, and cash flow tracking.

05 — Reports and Management Workflows

Executive sales reports, periodic balance sheets, profit/loss calculations, and administrative management workflows.

Note: This web application is a custom business operations and shop management platform for store operations (not a camera live-streaming or surveillance platform). Live production database access is restricted to authorized store management.
